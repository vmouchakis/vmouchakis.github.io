homelab-backup is a personal backup tool used only by its author, Vasilis Mouchakis.

- **Data accessed:** only the files and folders the tool creates in the author's own Google Drive (`drive.file` permission). It cannot see or read any other file.
- **Data collected:** none. Nothing is collected from, or about, any other person.
- **Data shared:** none. The backups go only to the author's own Google Drive.
- **Contact:** through the links on [this site's home page](/).
